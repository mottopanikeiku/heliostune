Limitations

These limitations are part of the typed claim record, not optional caveats.

  1. This is post-hoc exploratory descriptive evidence, not a confirmatory endpoint.
  2. The thresholds were written for this diagnostic and do not establish causality.
  3. Three calls reported one matching observed H100 profile/SKU and measured one fixed 96-workload corpus.
  4. The archived comparator selected Triton configurations on bank 1 and scored bank 2, while the probe reports three-bank medians.
  5. The probe cannot revise the frozen Hopper engineering STOP or any Parhelion claim.
